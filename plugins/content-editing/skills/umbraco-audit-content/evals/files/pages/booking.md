## Online booking that fills your calendar

Patients book in seconds from your website or a link in your emails. You choose which
appointment types can be booked online and how far ahead.

## How it works

1. Add the booking button to your website.
2. Patients pick a time that suits them.
3. The appointment lands in your calendar and the patient gets a confirmation.

## Calendar sync

Calendar sync works with Google Calendar and Outlook. Changes in either calendar show up within a
minute.

# Map content to blocks and write copy

Step 3 of umbraco-draft-page: turn the brief into the page plan.

## Mapping

- **Map before writing copy.** One plan row per block: purpose, block, fields, copy, source, open
  items. Write the copy only once the mapping makes sense.
- **Match the block to the content.** Long-form blocks (rich text) for explanation and argument;
  short-form blocks (cards, lists, stats) for short, parallel items that really are parallel.
- **Don't fill blocks for their own sake.** No stats block without real numbers, no testimonial
  without a real quote and permission, no extra blocks for visual variety.
- **When two blocks fit,** pick one and give the reason in one line.
- **When nothing fits,** add it to the plan's gaps: what the brief needs, why no block covers it,
  and the options (leave it out, use a simpler block, or ask a developer for a new block).

## Writing into fields

- **Field by field.** Respect every limit you could verify; mark the rest unverified.
- **The user's voice.** Follow their style guide if they have one; otherwise match the tone of the
  pages you read. Keep their product and feature names exactly.
- **Page fields count.** Write the SEO title and meta description for this page: what it is about
  and why a reader would click, unique to the page.
- **Links.** Use links from the brief or pages you confirmed exist. Mark unconfirmed URLs as open
  items.
- **Alt text** only when you know what the image shows. Otherwise list the image as an open item.
- **No placeholders in saved fields.** Never save "Lorem ipsum", "TBD" or "[image]". Leave the field
  empty and list it.

## Claims

Every factual claim (numbers, customers, prices, dates, capabilities) needs a source in the brief or
from the user. Put unsourced claims in the plan's "Claims to confirm" list and leave them out of the
copy until confirmed.
